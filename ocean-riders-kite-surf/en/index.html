Flat water

Why the lagoon, and not the sea.

Flat water is the strongest technical argument this school has, and the only one we can make without fabricating a fact. Lagoa da Conceição is enclosed, shallow in parts and steadier in wind — learning conditions. The swell, cross-shore wind and current of an open beach are exactly what gets in a beginner’s way.

The neighbourhood is a strong inference, not a verified fact: the lagoon is the island’s kite and SUP centre, and a Floripa kite school almost certainly bases there. [CONFIRM the exact meeting point, the water access and whether there is a physical base]

The progression, step by step.

  1. Theory and safety on landThe wind window, the safety system, the quick release, signals and right-of-way rules on the water.
  2. Flying the kite on the sandBar control, power and depower, assisted launch and landing.
  3. Body dragging in the waterMoving with the kite, recovering the board, self-rescue.

Safety and credentials

Seven empty fields, and each one is a lost booking.

Kitesurfing carries genuine injury risk. This section is not a badge row: it is the part of the page that makes a nervous beginner — or someone booking for an adult child — choose one school over another. Today none of it is written down anywhere.

  • Instructor certificationThe certifying body and each instructor’s credential number. No body they have not named themselves appears here — no logo, no acronym, no badge. [CONFIRM]
  • CADASTURMinistério do Turismo registration, which applies to tourism operators — and a kite school selling instruction to visitors generally is one. [CONFIRM the number, or written confirmation that they do not hold one]
  • Marinha do BrasilCommercial operation on the water and any support or rescue boat used during instruction fall under Brazilian Navy authority — vessel registration, mandatory safety equipment and a licensed operator, under the Normas da Autoridade Marítima.
