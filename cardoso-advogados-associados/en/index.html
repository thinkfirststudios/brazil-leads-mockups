What this site deliberately does not have

Every item below is standard on a US law-firm site and every one is prohibited or high-risk under the OAB’s rules. Their absence is a feature of the design, not an omission.

  • Testimonials, reviews or client ratings
  • Success rates, case counts or client counts
  • A “case results” or outcomes section
  • A free-consultation offer
  • Fees, price lists or “from R$…” pricing
  • Comparative or superlative language
  • A client logo wall
  • Animated counters and achievement badges
  • Countdowns or urgency devices
  • Exit-intent popups or “talk to a lawyer now” chat
