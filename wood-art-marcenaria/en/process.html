How we work with other trades

What a joinery workshop does not do

Saying this out loud prevents the most common scope argument in the trade, which is finding out on delivery day that the countertop was never included.

Stone countertops belong to the marmoraria

Granite, marble, quartz and a carved basin are another trade and another measurement — taken after the joinery is installed and levelled.

Glass and mirror belong to the vidraceiro

A glass door, a closet mirror and a glass shelf come through a specific supplier, with the measurement taken on site.

Upholstery belongs to the tapeceiro

An upholstered bedhead, a bench with a seat pad and a window cushion combine a joinery structure with an upholsterer’s covering.

Solid timber or MDF?

There is no single answer: solid timber moves with humidity and recovers with sanding; panel is stable and flat but lives or dies by its edge. Near the sea the decision changes weight — see the section on timber on the coast.

Does timber survive near the sea?

It does, if the specification is written for it: movement clearance, sealed edges, hardware with an adequate corrosion class and an air path behind the piece. Without that, salt air and the humidity swing collect in two or three years.

What is the warranty?

The statutory warranty is 90 days for durable goods and services, under CDC art. 26, II. Anything longer exists only if it is in writing (art. 50). The piece, the installation and the hardware are three separate warranties — the hardware’s is the manufacturer’s and carries its own conditions, which we have not read.
