⚠️ The award claims and the three certifications

This is the part that matters most in this conversation, and it is good news in disguise: they have substance to spare and are using the wrong words to describe it.

The awards

The current site says “the various awards received confirmed the quality and excellence of this small and charming ecological hotel” and refers to an “award-winning garden”. Not one award is named anywhere on the site. An award claim with no award, no body and no year is advertising that cannot be substantiated — exposure under art. 37 of the Brazilian consumer code. Neither line made it into this mockup. [CONFIRM which awards, who granted them and in what year — or drop both]

The certifications

The site cites three: the Programa de Certificação do Turismo Sustentável (with APEX support and BIRD funding), the Sistema de Gestão Sustentável (with the Instituto de Hospitalidade and the Programa Bem Receber), and the certificate against the sexual exploitation of children in tourism. None carries a certificate number or a validity date. Certification programmes lapse, and several of these belong to a phase of Brazilian tourism policy that has since changed.

So no mark is displayed in this mockup. All three appear as marked slots, and there is a switch at the top of script.js that controls it — it is off. [CONFIRM the number and validity of each, or remove them]

⚠️ The breakfast contradiction

Two pages of their site say different things about the same thing. The homepage calls the homemade breakfast a “signature”; the Sustainability page says “the homemade breakfast (temporarily suspended)”. One of them is wrong, and the person who finds out is the guest who has already booked. This mockup shows both, side by side, rather than picking one. [CONFIRM the current status]

The sustainability fee

They have charged a sustainability fee since October 2020, allocated 100% to sustainable purposes. It is good policy and a good selling point — and it is also a mandatory charge. Under the Brazilian consumer code and Decreto 5.903/2006, a mandatory amount must appear clearly before the booking closes, not as a surprise at check-in. What this site stores

One thing: tamarindo.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device. It reaches no server. The map in the location section is an SVG drawn here, not an embedded map, precisely so Google is not called without consent.

The forms

There are two: the availability enquiry and the events and retreats form. Both compose a message and hand it to WhatsApp; neither writes to a server and neither confirms a booking. Dates render as DD/MM. Events and nightly bookings have separate forms on purpose: they are different sales, with different timescales and different questions.

What this site does not claim

No rate, floor area, bed configuration, occupancy, sustainability fee amount, pet fee, distance in minutes, rating, review, award, current certification, CNPJ or CADASTUR number appears without being marked [CONFIRM]. The only year published is 1999, because it is theirs.

All the sustainability content is transcribed from their own page, word for word — nothing was added, and nothing needed to be: it is the most substantial documentation in this entire batch. The two diagrams (the irrigation loop and the ventilation axis) are drawings, because no photograph shows a closed loop or a wind axis. Every photograph is stock and the alt text on each one says it does not depict the lodge, the garden, the demolition-wood furniture or the team.
