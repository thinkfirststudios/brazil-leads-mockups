Buying property in Brazil as a foreigner

What actually happens

This is the exact question every one of these buyers has, it is completely absent from the current site, and in English it is uncontested organic search.

01

Get a CPF

A CPF is the Brazilian taxpayer number and you need one to hold title. You do not need residency to buy property in Brazil. It is issued by the Receita Federal, usually through a consulate.

02

Open a bank account or arrange remittance

You need a route for the funds. That is either a Brazilian account or a remittance arranged through a licensed institution.

03

Register the inbound funds with the Banco Central

This is the step that decides whether you can send the proceeds home when you sell. Money that arrives unregistered is difficult and expensive to repatriate later.

04

Due diligence

Certidões on the seller and the property, the matrícula and its full chain of title, IPTU and condomínio arrears — debts that follow the asset, not the seller.

05

Escritura and registration

The deed is signed before a notary at the cartório and the transfer is registered. Until it is registered, it has not happened.

Informational only — not legal or tax advice. A corretora is not a lawyer. Every step must be confirmed with a Brazilian real-estate lawyer.
