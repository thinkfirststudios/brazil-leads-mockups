What we found on opoke.com.br

The site exists. It just says nothing at all about what you sell

Checked 08/09. The page loads, it is WordPress with Elementor, and it contains exactly three things: the logo opoke.png (uploaded 10/2020), four social icons and an embedded Google Map. There is no menu, no price, no opening hours, no address in text, no bowl name, no roll name and not one line of indexable content. Someone landing on opoke.com.br cannot find out what a bowl costs, where the shop is, or when it opens. The only available action is to open WhatsApp and ask — which is what you have been doing, one customer at a time, for five years.
Two contradictions we have left visible rather than tidied away. The site title reads “Opoke – The Raw Bar”. The Google Maps name, in your own embed, reads “Opoke Sushi”.
