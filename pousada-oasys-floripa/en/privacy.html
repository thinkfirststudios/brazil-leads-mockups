Who the controller is

Controller: Pousada Oasys Floripa, Florianópolis (SC), Brazil. [CONFIRM the legal entity name, CNPJ, neighbourhood, full address and the data protection officer — LGPD art. 41]. There is no website, no published phone number and no published e-mail address. The only public channel identified is the Instagram profile @oasys.floripa, which sits behind a login wall and returns nothing to a server-side request.

The neighbourhood is not established either — the handle says only “floripa” — and it has not been inferred. The island is not one place, and guessing a district would change the rate expectation and the entire audience.

⚠️ No WhatsApp number was captured

There is no published phone number, WhatsApp or e-mail. Rather than drop in a stand-in number, every WhatsApp control in this build is visibly disabled and explains why when tapped. An invented number on a published site is not a layout detail: it is a stranger’s phone ringing. Confirm the number and the whole page works with no other change.

The Instagram feed is the most important section — and it still sits behind consent

Worth recording in writing, because it is a deliberate decision. The Instagram feed is the heart of this build: it sits high on the page, directly under the date picker, because today the feed is the business. And it still does not load on its own.

The Instagram embed is a third-party tracker: loading it hands your IP and an identifier to Meta before you do anything on the page. So it sits in the marketing category of the cookie gate and only arrives after consent; until then the section shows static stock images and no request leaves this page for Meta. Being the most important section exempts nobody from that. In this build the real embed is not even wired up.

The map in the location section, for the same reason, is an SVG drawn here — not an embedded map — so Google is not called without consent.

What this site stores

One thing: oasys.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device. It reaches no server and disappears when you clear site data.

The forms

There are two: the availability enquiry and the contact form. Both compose a text message and hand it to WhatsApp. Neither checks a calendar, neither writes to a server and neither confirms a booking. While there is no confirmed number they send nowhere at all: they show the notice above. Dates render as DD/MM.

On the availability enquiry, to be direct: until a booking engine is chosen it composes a WhatsApp message with the dates filled in. That is a legitimate route and it is what Brazilian guests already expect — but it is described as what it is, rather than pretending to be a live engine.

What this site does not claim

No rate, room name, room count, occupancy, bed configuration, amenity, pool, breakfast, parking, language spoken at reception, check-in time, cancellation policy, pet policy, distance, rating, review, award, founding year, CNPJ or CADASTUR number appears without being marked [CONFIRM].

The blanks are deliberate and part of the pitch: they show exactly what needs handing over, and what the page looks like once it is. For a pousada that sells itself on authenticity, that is worth more than a well-finished fiction. Every photograph is stock and the alt text on each one says it does not depict the pousada. No image shows children.
