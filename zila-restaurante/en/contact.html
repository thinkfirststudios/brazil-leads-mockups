What is the capacity of the space?

Can the whole space be hired?

Exclusive hire — a buyout — changes everything about the quote and the guest experience. [CONFIRM whether the space is hired exclusively or whether events run alongside normal service]

Does the restaurant close on event days?

A small question with a large frustration behind it: a guest arrives for dinner and finds a private party. [CONFIRM] Publishing it is the mark of a venue paying attention.
