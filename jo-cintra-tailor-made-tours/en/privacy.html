What the travel enquiry collects

The enquiry on this site collects dates or an intended month, party composition and the reason for the trip — honeymoon, celebration, family, corporate. That is not sensitive data in the sense of LGPD art. 11, but it is information a client considers personal, so the purpose and the retention period are stated here rather than left implicit. The data is used to design and answer that enquiry, and it reaches a named consultant, not a shared inbox. [CONFIRM the retention period].

Issuing travel services

On booking, we process the data required to issue tickets, reservations and insurance — which means transferring information to airlines, hotels, ground operators and insurers, in Brazil and abroad. That transfer is necessary to perform the contract and to meet the requirements of the destination country’s authorities (LGPD, arts. 7, V and 33).

The wedding gift registry

Foreign exchange

Foreign-exchange dealing in Brazil is regulated by the Banco Central and carried out through an authorised institution or as a correspondent of one. No FX service is advertised on this site until the authorisation or the partner institution is confirmed. [CONFIRM] — this is the highest-risk claim on the whole site.

Prices and currency

Nothing is publicly priced, and that is deliberate. Any figure that does appear will be in Brazilian reais (R$), with inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms stated. If a foreign-currency figure is quoted, the FX basis and date appear alongside it — the company runs a foreign-exchange desk, so this situation will arise.
