The space

A renovated house on the Campeche avenue

Espaço Valena opened on 08/12/2021, in a renovated house on Campeche’s main avenue in Florianópolis, joining a community known for its sustainability and active lifestyle.

“The place creates a feeling of calm and healing from the car park onwards.”

The house is a real differentiator and the current site never shows it. A health space in a house with a garden and its own parking is not the same thing as a room in an office block — and the photograph does that work on its own.
