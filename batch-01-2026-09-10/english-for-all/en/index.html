Level check

Two minutes, and nobody sees it.

The single biggest reason people do not start a course is not the price — it is not knowing their own level and being embarrassed to guess. Nobody walks into a school at 34 and announces they are a beginner.

So this check is private: no signup, no email, no published score and nothing stored. It runs in your browser and the result stays on your screen.

[CONFIRM] what does “família” mean here?

The only public signal the school has put into the world is the handle @familiaenglishforall — and it is genuinely ambiguous. It could mean (a) a family-run school; (b) a school that treats its student body as a família, a community; or (c) a school that teaches families together.

Those are three different websites. This mockup is built on the community reading, which is the safest and most broadly applicable — but the question is yours, and the answer changes the homepage, the photography and the voice.
