⚠ Medical questionnaire — sensitive health data

A diving operation collects a medical questionnaire and health declaration. That is sensitive personal data under art. 11 of the LGPD and requires specific, highlighted consent, restricted access and its own retention period.

So this website collects no health information at all. There is no medical form on any page, and the message field expressly asks you not to write health information there. The questionnaire is completed and signed at the school, on paper or in a dedicated system — never in a web field that lands in an inbox.
