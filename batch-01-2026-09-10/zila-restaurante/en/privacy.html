An event enquiry is a dossier

Name, WhatsApp, event type, date, guest count and budget range. Together those six fields describe a private occasion and a financial position — and they sit in an inbox until somebody answers.

So they are restricted to whoever builds the proposal, they are not exported to a marketing tool, a shared spreadsheet or a messaging group, and they do not feed ad segmentation. An enquiry that did not become a contract is deleted within the period stated below [CONFIRM the period].

The date is the delicate part. “Wedding, 14/03, 120 guests” is not a CRM field: it is a fact about somebody’s life, often months before they tell the rest of the world.

Guest lists: third-party data

When an event has a guest list, the buyer hands over other people’s names and, often, their dietary restrictions. Each guest remains a data subject: they can ask for access, correction and deletion directly, and the buyer does not waive those rights on their behalf. The list is used only for the event and deleted afterwards [CONFIRM the period].

Event photography

Photographing a private event inside the venue and posting it afterwards requires specific written permission, separate from the booking contract, stating where the image may appear and for how long. That counts double where minors are present: a child’s image requires the guardians’ consent (LGPD art. 14) and does not go into commercial material without it.
