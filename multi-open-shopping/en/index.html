Cult, children’s and commercial programming. The cinema runs on its own domain, and that is where the programme is maintained.

See the programme at cinemulti.com.br

No showtime has been copied here — and that is the most important design decision in this section. The current homepage duplicates the showtimes by hand and the block has gone stale: it lists films from an earlier season, one of them with a typo in the title, directly under November news. Anyone who notices that starts doubting the opening hours too. Live information either comes from a single maintained source with a visible update stamp, or it does not belong on the homepage. Here it leaves the homepage and becomes a link to the source. [CONFIRM whether a showtimes feed exists that could be consumed]

About MULTI

Opened on 23 March 2018.

The first and only open-air shopping centre in the south of the island of Santa Catarina — their words. Three blocks, 155 suites in the Offices, a praça for open-air events, an auditorium, and a terrace with a privileged view toward Ilha do Campeche.

Virtual tour

One word comes out. Their meta description says “the first and best open-air shopping centre in the south of the island”. “First” is a date claim and verifiable. “Best” is an unsubstantiable superiority claim, and under the consumer code and CONAR norms a comparative claim must be provable. It has not been reproduced in this build. [CONFIRM whether they can substantiate it]
