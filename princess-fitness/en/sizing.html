How to measure

  1. Use a tape measure over skin or a thin garment, without pulling tight.
  2. Bust: around the fullest part, tape level.
  3. Underbust: just below, where the top elastic sits.
  4. Waist: at the narrowest part of the torso.
  5. Hip: around the fullest part, feet together.

Coverage scale, by bottom shape

  • String (fio) — Minimal coverage
  • Tanga — Medium coverage
  • Asa delta (high-leg V) — Medium coverage
  • High waist — High coverage
  • Hot pants — High coverage

Support scale, by top shape

  • Cortininha (ruched triangle) — Light support
  • Triangle — Light support
  • Bandeau — Medium support
  • Half-cup — Firm support
  • Sports top — High support
