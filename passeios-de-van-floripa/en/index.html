What we found on the current site
Your website has two pages
That is not a figure of speech. The pages-sitemap.xml at passeiosdevanfloripa.com.br contains exactly two addresses: the homepage and /contato. Everything you sell — transfers, private hires, corporate work, excursions — is an anchor inside one long Wix scroll. There is nothing for Google to rank, nothing to link to and nothing to send anyone.
And your address is the second story. Av. das Nações, 192, Canasvieiras. Canasvieiras is the neighbourhood where Argentine and Uruguayan holidaymakers stay — in season, Spanish is the working language of that commercial strip. You are headquartered in the middle of the largest Spanish-speaking inbound market in southern Brazil, running airport transfers, with a monolingual Portuguese site and a WhatsApp link on the page that is missing its country code (wa.me/48999900608), so it fails for exactly the international phones it needs to work for. In this mockup the link is corrected everywhere.
Somebody typing Florianopolis airport transfer has a flight booked, a hotel booked, and three questions: can you get me to my hotel, what does it cost, and will you be there if my flight lands at midnight? The current site answers none of them. This mockup answers all three — and leaves visible, marked [CONFIRM], everything only you can fill in.
