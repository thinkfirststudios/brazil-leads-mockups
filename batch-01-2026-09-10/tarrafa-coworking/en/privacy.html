Biometrics: sensitive data, its own regime

Tarrafa’s 24/7 access works by biometric reading. Under the LGPD, biometric data is sensitive personal data (art. 5, II) and may only be processed with specific, highlighted consent (art. 11, I) — never buried in the plan contract or in a general clause.

In practice that means a separate authorisation stating what is collected, where it is stored, who has access, how long it is kept and what happens when the plan ends.
