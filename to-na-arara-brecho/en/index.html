Condition

The scale, published

Almost no brechó in Brazil publishes a condition scale. Publishing one does three things at once: it wins trust from people buying unseen, it cuts returns, and it is the difference between “a pile of second-hand clothes” and “a selection”.

The rule that makes the scale real: every piece below As New carries a close-up photograph of the mark. No exceptions. Wear that was described and photographed is not a defect; wear that was not, is — and the CDC art. 26 legal warranty applies to second-hand goods just the same.
This scale is a proposal, not yours. The five points have to match how the owner actually sorts pieces, because a scale she cannot apply every day does not survive a fortnight. [CONFIRM the real grades and the words she already uses]
New and used cannot mix without a label. Secondary sources say the shop sells both. If it does, every piece has to say which — it changes the price expectation, it changes the warranty position, and trust in everything else on the page depends on it. It is the single largest consumer-law exposure in this business model. [CONFIRM]

Shipping, exchanges and returns

The one-of-one problem, faced head on

This is where most brechós copy a normal shop’s text and promise something they cannot deliver.

Exchanging for another size is structurally impossible

There is no second unit. Copying a normal retailer’s exchange policy is promising what cannot be delivered. What genuinely exists is a refund, or credit toward another piece — and the site has to say so in those words. [CONFIRM which]

Right of regret — 7 days (CDC art. 49)

Every remote purchase gives seven calendar days from receipt to cancel, for any reason, with a full refund including outbound shipping. That applies to a brechó just the same. It belongs on the piece tag and at reservation, not only inside a policy page.

Legal warranty — on second-hand goods too (CDC art. 26)

Wear that was described and photographed is not a defect. Wear that was not described is. That is exactly why the condition scale and the flaw photograph are compliance infrastructure, not a design flourish.

One question for the first conversation

The lead list describes Tô na Arara as a “curated upscale vintage and independent fashion boutique”. Those are two different businesses, with two different photographic registers, two price framings and two different websites.
