Gluten, allergens, and what is not claimed

This site publishes no product as “gluten free”. Kompaan works with wheat and rye in one room, which makes cross-contamination a physical reality until a separate, verified production area exists. Gluten labelling is mandatory under Lei 10.674/2003 and allergen declaration under ANVISA RDC 26/2015 — both apply to every item sold.
