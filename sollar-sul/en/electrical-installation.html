Safety and compliance

Why you should demand an ART from your installer

This is not a legal disclaimer — it is the strongest sales argument this business has, because a good deal of the competition issues no ART at all.

NBR 5410

NBR 5410

The Brazilian standard for low-voltage electrical installations. It is what separates an installation that is properly done from one that merely works.

ART

ART

The formal record of technical responsibility, issued by a CREA-registered professional (Lei 6.496/1977). It is the document that says who is technically answerable for the work.

What happens without an ART

Warranty

There are three separate warranties

Legal warranty — 90 days

Durable goods and services carry a 90-day statutory warranty from delivery (CDC art. 26, II). It applies whether it is written down or not.

Installation warranty

Any longer period is contractual and must be given in writing (CDC art. 50). [CONFIRM the terms]

The number that changes everything is on your electricity bill

Solar is the one service on this site where a form beats WhatsApp, because the quote needs structured input. The field that matters is average consumption in kWh.

Where to find it: on the CELESC bill, look for the 12-month consumption history and take the average in kWh. That number is the difference between a real quote and a guess.

A prior written estimate itemising labour, materials and dates (CDC art. 40), binding for 10 days from receipt unless otherwise agreed — and nothing outside it can be charged without authorisation.
What about uploading the bill? An electricity bill carries a name, an address, an installation number and a consumption pattern — personal data with real weight. So this mockup does not implement an upload: the safer path is to send a photo on WhatsApp, and if an upload really is wanted it needs a stated purpose, a retention period and a deletion route in the policy.
