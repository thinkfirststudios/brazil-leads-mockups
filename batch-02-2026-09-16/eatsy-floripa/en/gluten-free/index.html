Gluten at EATSY: what we know and what we can’t guarantee

Before looking at dishes with gluten-free ingredients, please read the cross-contact notice below.

Gluten and cross-contact information

What our kitchen can — and cannot — guarantee

EATSY does not use wheat in its preparations, but gluten cross-contact is present in the kitchen. “Gluten-free” on a dish describes its ingredients, not the kitchen. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free from gluten. If you have coeliac disease or gluten sensitivity, please take this into account before ordering and speak to the team. [CONFIRM precisely, in the client’s own words — identical text in the notice and the FAQ]

This applies to the whole menu, to delivery and to pre-orders.

  • Wheat: not used in preparations [CONFIRM]
  • Dedicated gluten-free area: none [CONFIRM]
  • Utensils, benches and frying: shared [CONFIRM]
  • Per-dish labelling: “CONTÉM GLÚTEN” or “NÃO CONTÉM GLÚTEN” (Brazilian Law 10.674/2003) [CONFIRM per dish]

Allergens & information

Allergens, gluten and labelling

Allergens (ANVISA RDC 26/2015)

Per-dish declaration for wheat (gluten), milk, eggs, soy, tree nuts, peanuts and sesame, where applicable.

Watch for nuts and sesame in toppings, plant-based “cheeses” and tahini dressings. [CONFIRM per dish]

Gluten (Law 10.674/2003)

Every dish is labelled “CONTÉM GLÚTEN” (contains gluten) or “NÃO CONTÉM GLÚTEN” (gluten-free).

“Gluten-free” refers to ingredients — see the cross-contact notice.
