Four forms, four purposes, four retention rules

Today a visitor, a prospective tenant, an event organiser and an advertiser all land in the same form. They are not the same question, and under the LGPD they are not the same processing either. In this build each has its own stated purpose and retention rule:

  • Visitor — name, e-mail, subject and message. Legal basis: consent. Short retention, because the purpose is exhausted by the reply. [CONFIRM the period]
  • Suite leasing — name, company, activity, area wanted, WhatsApp and message. Legal basis: pre-contractual dealings alongside consent. [CONFIRM the period]
  • Event — name, type of event, preferred date, number of people and message. Legal basis: pre-contractual dealings. [CONFIRM the period]
  • Advertising — name, company, format of interest and message. Legal basis: pre-contractual dealings. [CONFIRM the period]

The directory publishes third parties’ data

The directory lists the name, suite number, phone, website and individual hours of businesses that are not the controller. Each tenant needs a documented legal basis for that publication and a clear route to correct or remove their own entry. [CONFIRM the collection, approval and removal process]. The entries in this build carry only what their own site already publishes; nothing has been added, no logo has been used and no tenant has been invented. Using any tenant’s logo depends on that tenant’s permission.

Live information is consumer information

Opening hours and cinema showtimes displayed on a site are consumer information. A stale showtimes block that sends someone on a nine-kilometre drive is an information failure as well as a credibility one — it costs trust in everything else on the page, the hours included. So in this build every live-information block carries a visible “updated DD/MM” stamp, or it leaves the homepage and becomes a link to the source that is actually maintained. No showtime has been copied here.

Sustainability

The environmental information on this site is reproduced exactly as the development publishes it — the sewage treatment plant, 11,000 litres of rainwater capture, no plastic straws or cups in the food operations, organic-waste composting, the solar system in partnership with ElektSolar Innovations, two bike racks and one EV charging space. Nothing has been rounded, nothing added, and no blanket “sustainable” or “carbon neutral” label applied, because they do not use one. An unsubstantiated environmental claim is exposure under the consumer code.

Commercial leasing

Leasing the suites is governed by the Lei do Inquilinato (Lei 8.245/91), not the Brazilian consumer code. Any advertised area, rate or availability must be accurate, and the rate has to say what it covers — rent, service charge, IPTU or the total. None of it is published today, so everything in this build is a placeholder.

Images

Every photograph is stock, used as a placeholder, and none shows children. That matters here in particular: the tenant mix includes a children’s ballet school and a baby-and-kids retailer, and the seasonal programme includes Father Christmas. None of those is illustrated with an image of a child in this build, and photography of children only enters from the development itself, with documented parental permission.
