Their public handle begins with a franchise prefix. If the affiliation is live, the controller of booking data may not be the pousada alone: a franchise platform typically runs the booking engine, holds the guest data and sets the policy. That changes the content of this entire page. [CONFIRM the franchise relationship and, if there is one, who controls booking data and under whose policy].

Until that is clarified, this policy describes only what this site does, which is little, and deliberately so.

⚠️ No WhatsApp number was captured — and none has been invented

There is no phone number, no WhatsApp and no e-mail. Rather than drop in a stand-in number, every WhatsApp control in this build is visibly disabled and explains why when tapped. An invented number on a published site is not a layout detail: it is a stranger’s phone ringing. Confirm the number and the whole page works with no other change.

What this site stores

One thing: bangalos.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device. It reaches no server and disappears when you clear site data.

The Instagram feed sits behind consent

The Instagram embed is a third-party tracker: loading it hands your IP and an identifier to Meta before you do anything at all. So it sits in the marketing category and does not load without consent. Until then the section shows static stock images and no request leaves this page for Meta. In this build the real embed is not even wired up.

The forms

There are two: the availability enquiry and the contact form. Both compose a text message and hand it to WhatsApp. Neither checks a calendar, neither writes to a server and neither confirms a booking. While there is no confirmed number they send nowhere at all: they show the notice above. Dates render as DD/MM.

Rates, and the deliberate silence about them

No rate appears in this build because none is published. One more thing worth recording: nowhere have we written that booking direct is cheaper. Franchise and OTA agreements commonly impose rate parity and forbid advertising a lower direct rate; such a line going live can be a breach. It only ships after [CONFIRM the contractual freedom to make it].

No bungalow count, unit type, occupancy, bed configuration, rate, distance in metres or minutes, amenity, check-in time, cancellation policy, pet policy, language spoken at reception, founding year, CNPJ or CADASTUR number appears without being marked [CONFIRM].
