Right of regret — 7 days (CDC art. 49)

A purchase made away from the premises gives the customer seven calendar days from receipt to cancel, for any reason, with a full refund of everything paid — outbound shipping included. It is not a courtesy, it is law, and it has to be stated in plain language on the product page and at checkout, not only inside a policy page.

Legal warranty — 30 and 90 days (CDC art. 26)

Thirty days for non-durable goods and ninety for durable ones, against defects. It is separate from the goodwill exchange policy, and the goodwill policy must not be presented as if it were the legal minimum.
