The sports

What gets played here

Beach tennis

In Floripa beach tennis is not a novelty — it is a mass-participation sport with a circuit, a ranking and fixed pairs. Played in doubles on sand with a short racket and a depressurised ball; the point ends when the ball touches the sand. The island has become one of the centres of the sport in Brazil.

Footvolley

Volleyball played without hands, on sand, in pairs. It demands touch, timing and a good foot — and it is the fastest-growing sport on the island's courts after beach tennis.
