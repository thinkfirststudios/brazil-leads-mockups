Two forms, two purposes, two retention rules

This site has two separate forms, and that is not a layout detail: a leasing prospect and a shopper are not the same enquiry, and today both land in the same inbox. Each has its own stated purpose and its own retention rule.

The professional directory publishes third parties’ data

The directory lists the name, speciality, suite, phone, hours and professional registration of people who are not the controller. Each tenant needs a documented legal basis for that publication and a clear route to correct or remove their own entry.

Beyond the LGPD, a directory listing a doctor, dentist, psychologist, lawyer or architect touches professional-council advertising rules — CFM/CRM, CFO/CRO, CFP/CRP, OAB and CAU — several of which require the registration number to appear beside the name. In this build no registration number has been invented, no promotional copy has been written on anyone’s behalf, and there is no superlative and no outcome claim on any card. The directory publishes only what each professional supplies and approves. [CONFIRM the collection, approval and removal process].

Commercial leasing is not a consumer relationship

Leasing the suites is governed by the Lei do Inquilinato (Lei 8.245/91), not the Brazilian Consumer Code. Any advertised rate, area or availability must be accurate, and the rate has to say what it covers — rent, service charge, IPTU or the total. The consumer code still applies to the retail side: a shop or food-court price must be accurate and in reais.

Accessibility

As a publicly accessible commercial building and an address housing consulting rooms, NBR 9050 applies: accessible entrance, lifts, accessible toilets and accessible bays.
