FAQ

The FAQ that currently 404s

The frequently-asked-questions link on the live site returns “Pagina nao encontrada!”. This is the rebuilt FAQ — and the old address needs a 301 on migration day.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 14:00, check-out until 12:00. Since there is no reception, you let yourself in with your code whenever you arrive after that.

How does the access code work?

Every guest gets their own code. Which channel it is sent through and how far ahead [CONFIRM].

Are there lockers?

Yes. At check-in you get a key for an individual, private space for your things. Locker size and whether a large backpack fits [CONFIRM].

Do the two buildings work the same way?

Shared dorms are at 2826 and private rooms at 2616, on the same road. Whether access, the code and the times are identical at both [CONFIRM].

Joaquina is the female-only dorm. Who may book it, how that is enforced in practice and whether it has its own bathroom [CONFIRM the written policy].

Does R$ 33 a night apply to any stay?

No. R$ 33 is the shared-dorm rate at 29 nights or more. Each figure in the table applies only inside its own band, and Smart Hostel's own caveat says special dates, high season and public holidays may change it.
