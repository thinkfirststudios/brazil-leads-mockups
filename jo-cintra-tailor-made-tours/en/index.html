Jo Cintra

Four decades in travel before this agency existed.

“Mais do que planejar viagens, cultivamos relações, conhecimento e experiências construídas ao longo de décadas.”

She started in a travel agency at sixteen. She worked through every stage of the sector, from operations to full itinerary planning. She interned at an airline. She moved to Florianópolis. Later she lived in London, which “consolidated her global outlook”. She kept the business trading through crises and technological change — and bet on personalised travel built on relationship, close listening and specialist curation.

Every sentence above is quoted from your own page. Nothing has been added — and that is exactly why it works. This story is the strongest asset on the site and today it sits three clicks away, behind a nav item called “Sobre”.
