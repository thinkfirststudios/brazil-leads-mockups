The WhatsApp conversation is the intake form

The booking path here is WhatsApp, which means the first thing a new person does is describe a symptom in an app. Worth saying out loud: from the moment the message is sent, the processing of that data inside the app is also governed by Meta Platforms’ policy, over which we have no control.

So the practical guidance is: send enough to book, and leave the clinical detail for the assessment with the professional. How long conversations are kept and on which device [CONFIRM].

Clinical records

A treatment record in a health establishment has its own retention period, set by the professional council of whoever provides the care, and it cannot be deleted on request while that period runs — that is a legal obligation, not our choice. What this site does not publish, and why

There is no before-and-after photograph anywhere, no gallery, no slider and no space held for one — CFM Resolution 1.974/2011 prohibits before/after imagery in medical advertising in Brazil. There is also no price, promotion or discount on any service, under the same resolution, until it is confirmed which offerings are medical procedures and which are wellness services.

And no image on this site is a Valena room, professional or client: every one is stock and labelled as such. No face appears beside a treatment name, because a face beside a treatment is read as a result.

Professional registrations

A health establishment must display its responsible professional and that person’s council registration, as well as a current municipal health licence [CONFIRM].
