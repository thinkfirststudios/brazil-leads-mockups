Buying in Florianópolis: the process

Property record and certificates

The up-to-date matrícula shows the owner, the property description and any encumbrances; certificates on the seller and the property complete the check.

ITBI transfer tax

A municipal tax, as a rule paid by the buyer before registration. Current rate [CONFIRM].

Deed and registration

The public deed is signed at a notary's office; ownership transfers once it is registered on the matrícula.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreign national buy property in Florianópolis?

As a rule, foreign individuals can acquire urban property in Brazil and need a Brazilian taxpayer number (CPF), among other requirements. Tax, currency-exchange and immigration matters must be handled by the appropriate professionals.

What is ITBI?

ITBI is the municipal property-transfer tax, as a rule paid by the buyer before the deed is registered.

What is the difference between the deed and registration?

The public deed (escritura) is signed at a notary's office; ownership only passes to the buyer once the deed is registered on the property record (matrícula) at the Real Estate Registry.

Are prices shown in US dollars?

No. All prices are in Brazilian reais (R$), on every language version of this site.

How long does a purchase take?
