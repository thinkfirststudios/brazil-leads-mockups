Edge profiles

The profiles, with their proper names.

Pure information, no prices. It is the vocabulary a customer needs before asking for a quote.

  • SquareThe plain vertical face. The most direct profile and the most common.
  • MitredTwo pieces joined at 45°, giving a thicker front without the extra weight.
  • BullnoseA rounded arris, softer to the touch and less prone to chipping.
  • ChamferA straight cut on the lower arris. Discreet.
  • BevelA pronounced chamfer on the upper arris, which throws light back.

Safety and quality

Wet cutting, extraction and PPE.

Silica dust is the occupational risk that defines this trade, and almost no marmoraria in the region says a word about it. A workshop that cuts wet and controls the dust has a good story to tell — and it reads as professionalism, not as a warning.

Nothing has been published here about Sul da Ilha’s practice. In Brazil this sits under NR-15 Anexo 12 (exposure limits for free crystalline silica) and NR-01/NR-09 (PGR, a mandatory employer programme). No certification, programme or practice they may not have is claimed. [CONFIRM their dust-control practice and PGR status before a single line is published]
