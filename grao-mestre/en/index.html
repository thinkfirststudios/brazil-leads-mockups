Where it comes from

For a natural food store, provenance is the whole credibility argument.

[CONFIRM fornecedores, produtores locais, certificação orgânica — nada é afirmado sem comprovação]

“Orgânico” is a regulated term. It appears nowhere here without valid certification, and certification is never implied. The same discipline covers “integral”, “sem açúcar” and “funcional”: state only what the supplier’s own label states. And no health claims: nothing on this site says a product treats, prevents, slims or strengthens — ANVISA restricts that tightly and a mockup must not model the wrong habit.

Allergens, labelling & information

Two different obligations, because this is two businesses.

The buffet — ANVISA RDC 26/2015

Mandatory per-dish allergen declaration, for those that apply:

wheat (gluten)milkeggs soytree nutspeanutssesame

Cross-contact — standing declaration: a buffet is a shared serving environment. Utensils, steam and the closeness of adjacent trays make cross-contact inherent, and that has to be stated plainly — not hidden in a footnote. [CONFIRM]

“Contém glúten” / “Não contém glúten” per Law 10.674/2003, per dish. [CONFIRM]

The store, sold loose — labelling at point of sale

  • A product sold loose is still required to make allergen and origin information available at the point of sale. [CONFIRM]
  • The risk of cross-contact between bins — nuts and gluten-containing grains especially — is real and must be disclosed. [CONFIRM]
  • Never restate a nutritional or allergen claim beyond what the supplier’s own label says. Photograph or link the label rather than paraphrasing it.
  • Price per unit of weight, always visible and legible (CDC).

If you ever ship products

Distance selling triggers the CDC: a 7-day right of withdrawal (art. 49), a stated delivery timeframe and full supplier identification. That is a phase two with its own scope — not a shop that quietly appears on this site. [CONFIRM]
