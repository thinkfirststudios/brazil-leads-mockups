the most important line on this site “100% plant-based” is a claim, not a decoration — which is why it is switched off in this build. It goes live only once the kitchen confirms that the bread, the sauces, the fryer oil and every ingredient are free of animal products, and states whether the kitchen also handles non-vegan items. Someone with a dairy or egg allergy and someone with an ethical commitment both rely on that sentence — and a false one is misleading advertising under the Brazilian Consumer Code.

about these photographs Every burger photograph on this site is stock, and every one of them shows meat and dairy cheese — there is no decent stock photography of a plant-based burger. In a 100% plant-based kitchen that is not an aesthetic detail: it is the difference between showing your product and showing precisely what you do not serve. Real photography, shot on black, is not an extra on this project — it is a requirement.

Not one burger, ingredient, sauce or price on this menu is real. On a burger menu the temptation to write something plausible is high and the damage is total: someone choosing on an ingredient list cannot be handed an invented one. The whole board comes from a single array at the top of script.js — editable live in the meeting.

allergens & choices

plant-based does not mean allergen-free.

Soy and nuts are in almost every plant-based patty and cheese. A plant-based menu does not reduce the allergen burden — it shifts it. Which is why the useful filter here is not “vegan”: it is which allergen this item avoids.
