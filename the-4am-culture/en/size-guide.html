Shipping and returns

Said plainly, above the footer

This is a conversion section, not a legal one. Clarity here reduces cart abandonment directly.

Free shipping over R$ 600,00

This threshold is yours and it is a genuine, non-eroding incentive. One thing is missing: is it calculated before or after discounts? [CONFIRM]

Right of regret — 7 days (CDC art. 49)

A purchase made away from the premises gives the customer seven calendar days from receipt to cancel, for any reason, with a full refund of everything paid — outbound shipping included. It is not a courtesy, it is law, and it has to be stated in plain language on the product page and at checkout, not only inside a policy page.

Legal warranty — 30 and 90 days (CDC art. 26)

Thirty days for non-durable goods and ninety for durable ones, against defects. It is separate from the goodwill exchange policy, and the goodwill policy must not be presented as if it were the legal minimum.

FAQ

What people ask before buying

Which line should I choose?

It depends what you are going to do. Compress to train with everything held in place, Soft to stretch as if you were wearing nothing, Dry to sweat, Fresh+ to give everything in any activity, and Pump to lift the body and the look. All five lines are theirs, and the comparison table above exists precisely for this.

How do the sizes run?

Size run, measurements in centimetres and a between-sizes recommendation [CONFIRM]. The measurement table is built and empty on purpose — no measurement has been invented.

Are the light colours see-through?

That is the most-asked question about light-coloured leggings, and the honest answer is worth more than the flattering one. Opacity changes by line and by colour. [CONFIRM line by line] — nothing has been asserted here.

How long does delivery take?

Times by region, carrier and tracking [CONFIRM]. What is certain is free shipping over R$ 600,00, which they publish — what is missing is whether that is calculated before or after discounts.

Can I exchange for another size?

Yes, with a window, a garment-condition requirement and a return-postage rule [CONFIRM]. And, separately from that, there is the 7-day right of regret (CDC art. 49), which applies for any reason and refunds the outbound shipping too.

Do you ship outside Brazil?

The site’s country selector lists about forty countries. If those orders are actually fulfilled, the site has to state international delivery times, who pays import duty and whether returns from abroad are possible. [CONFIRM]

Do you accept Pix?

The payment methods published in their footer today are American Express, Diners Club, Discover, Elo, Hypercard, JCB, Mastercard and Visa. Pix does not appear. In Brazil, in 2026, that is unusual enough to be worth asking directly. [CONFIRM whether Pix is accepted at checkout and simply not shown in the icons]
