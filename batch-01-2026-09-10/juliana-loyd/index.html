Who I work with

You will recognise yourself in one of these.

People book when they see themselves described, not when they read a list of features.

The professional who reads fluently but freezes in meetings

You understand every email. You follow the documentation. Then the call starts and the words stop arriving. This is the most common thing I work on, and it is a speaking problem, not a knowledge problem.

The newcomer who needs English for work in a Brazilian company

You have moved here, the company works in English with clients abroad, and you need to hold your own in that room from next month, not next year.

The person who studied for years and still cannot hold a conversation

Years of courses, grammar you can recite, and a conversation that dies in thirty seconds. We start from what you can already do, and build outward from there.

The parent who needs English for their own life

School meetings, forms, doctors, the other parents. Practical, unglamorous English for the situations you are actually in.

Under the CDC, a package has to state the number of lessons, the validity period, and the cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms. Package expiry is the single most common dispute in private tutoring — saying it plainly protects her, not just the student. A trial lesson is only advertised as free if it genuinely is.

If she would rather discuss price in a message, this section says so plainly — “every student’s schedule is different, message me and I’ll send you options” — rather than omitting the topic. Omitting it is what makes people book on a marketplace instead.
