Rental guarantees

How to secure a rental in Brazil

Brazil's Tenancy Law (Law 8.245/91) allows only one type of guarantee per contract. Which types each property accepts is up to the owner.

Guarantor (fiador)

A person who, under the contract, takes on the tenant's obligations if the tenant does not meet them. Proof of income and assets is usually required. Requirements: [CONFIRM].

Deposit (caução)

Paid by the tenant at the start; a cash deposit is limited by law to three months' rent. It is returned at the end, as set out in the contract.

Guarantee insurance

A policy issued by an insurer, subject to a background check. Who pays the premium, on which properties and for what term: [CONFIRM].

Capitalisation bond

The tenant buys a bond for the amount set in the contract; it stays linked to the tenancy and is redeemable at the end. Accepted amounts: [CONFIRM].

FAQ

Common questions

Which documents do I need to buy property in Brazil?

Generally: personal documents for buyer and seller, the buyer's CPF (Brazilian taxpayer number), an up-to-date property registration certificate (matrícula) and clearance certificates for the seller and the property. The final list depends on each case.

What are ITBI and the escritura?

ITBI is the municipal property transfer tax. The escritura is the public deed signed at a notary office (Tabelionato de Notas); it is then registered on the matrícula, which is what transfers ownership.

Which rental guarantees are accepted?

Brazil's Tenancy Law (8.245/91, art. 37) provides for a deposit, a personal guarantor, rent-guarantee insurance and fiduciary assignment of investment fund units. Only one type is allowed per contract.

Who pays condominium fees and IPTU?

Under Law 8.245/91, ordinary condominium expenses are paid by the tenant and extraordinary ones by the owner. IPTU (property tax) is the owner's, unless the contract expressly transfers it to the tenant.
