Accessibility

Adapted suites — their own words, brought forward.

  • Located in the Hotel Internacional
  • Bathroom with no shower box and a wider entry door
  • Grab bars and a shower chair
  • Elevator access to the suites
  • Access ramps into the Hotel Internacional

A resort that publicly advertises adapted suites is holding itself to an accessibility standard — so the site must meet the same one (eMAG / WCAG 2.1 AA), and this copy deserves to be a real page rather than a footnote.

Renovation notice they publish themselves: “during the renovation period, from 03/05/2026 to 19/09/2026, the adapted accommodations remain available”. Today that notice is buried inside an accessibility paragraph where no booker will find it. Here it sits where the decision is made. [CONFIRM] current status.
