Our Commitment
The most important section on the site, and it ships blank
This is the page that justifies the rate. On an OTA listing an eco pousada is flattened into the same checkboxes as everybody else — Wi-Fi, air conditioning, parking — and there is nowhere at all to say why it costs more. ⚠ Why this list is empty. In Brazil an environmental claim is regulated conduct: under the consumer code and CONAR’s rules it must be specific, truthful and substantiable. “Ecological” and “sustainable” with no named practice behind them are exactly what gets challenged. Since nothing could be verified, not one line was written — no “carbon neutral”, no “100% renewable”, no “zero waste”, no certification named, no tree count. Writing any of them would hand you a liability, not a favour. Fill it in and this section becomes the most valuable asset on the site.
