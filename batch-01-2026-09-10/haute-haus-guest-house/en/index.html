Who we are

A reinterpretation of the beach-house style with a modern, minimalist twist.

“A Haute Haus é uma charmosa Guest House localizada às margens da Lagoa da Conceição. Seu projeto arquitetônico é uma releitura do estilo praiano com um twist moderno e minimalista. Possuímos 8 suítes duplas, algumas com vista para a Lagoa, e outras com vista para a piscina. São 2 hectares de muita vegetação e contato com a natureza, além de um lounge à beira da piscina.”

Their words, reproduced verbatim. That sentence is the whole brand in one line — and it is the design brief for this site.

They describe themselves as a Guest House — not a pousada, not a hotel. The distinction is deliberate and is held throughout the site, including in the schema (BedAndBreakfast) and in the titles.

The grounds

2 ha

Two hectares for eight suites.

Twenty thousand square metres of planting for sixteen guests. It is an out-of-scale ratio for a lodging — and on the current site it appears exactly once, in a subordinate clause, in the middle of a list of facilities.

Bicycles

Free bicycles for guests — and not a word about it on the site.

Guest reviews mention free bicycles and the closeness of Avenida das Rendeiras again and again. Your own amenity copy never mentions either. It is a genuine, charming courtesy that is invisible to anyone who has not stayed yet. [CONFIRM]

Hospitality

“Praticando a arte de receber bem.”

The published reviews describe a small, present team and a mother-and-daughter pair working on site. It is the most-cited reason people return.

The reviews name staff members. No employee name is published on this site without documented consent — not in a testimonial, not in a photo caption. And no review here points at a platform: the ones on the current site carry no stated source, so there is nothing to attribute. Either they are pulled live from a named source, or they are presented anonymised — and no score is asserted.
