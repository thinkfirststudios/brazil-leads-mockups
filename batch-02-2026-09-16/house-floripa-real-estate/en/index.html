Step by step

Buying property in Florianópolis — the process

A short, neutral explainer for buyers from abroad. Every step must be checked by a Brazilian real-estate lawyer before publication. [CONFIRM with lawyer]

For legal, tax or currency matters, we introduce you to an independent professional. [CONFIRM whether a partner lawyer (OAB) or despachante is involved]

Full process page →

  1. 01

    CPF

    A CPF (Brazilian individual taxpayer number) is required to buy property in Brazil. Foreigners can obtain one, and buying does not require residency. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

  2. 02

    Bank account or remittance

    Payment is made through an account at a Brazilian bank or by a remittance from abroad through an authorised institution. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

  3. 03

    Registering the funds with the Central Bank

    Funds brought in from abroad are registered with the Banco Central do Brasil, so that sale proceeds can later be repatriated. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

  4. 04

    Due diligence

    Review of certificates (certidões), the property registry record (matrícula) and any outstanding IPTU property tax or condominium fees. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

  5. 05

    ITBI

    The municipal property-transfer tax, paid before registration. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

  6. 06

    Deed and registration

    The public deed (escritura pública) is signed at a notary (tabelionato) and then registered at the Property Registry. In Brazil, ownership only passes on registration. [CONFIRM]

    ⓘInformational only — not legal or tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a CPF to buy property?
Yes. The CPF is the Brazilian taxpayer number and is required for the purchase. Foreigners can apply for one. [CONFIRM wording]
Do I need to live in Brazil to buy?
No residency is required to buy property in Brazil. Visa and residency questions are for an independent immigration professional.
What is the remittance registration?
Funds sent from abroad are registered with the Banco Central do Brasil, which allows proceeds to be sent back later. [CONFIRM with lawyer]
What is a cartório?
A Brazilian notary or registry office. The deed is signed at a notary (tabelionato de notas) and registered at the Property Registry (Registro de Imóveis).
What documents are needed?
Usually an ID or passport, a CPF and supporting documents; for the property, an updated registry record and certificates. [CONFIRM list]
What is ITBI?
The municipal property-transfer tax, paid before registration. Rate: [CONFIRM with Florianópolis city hall]
What is the difference between the deed and registration?
The public deed is drawn up at a notary; ownership only transfers once it is registered at the Property Registry.
