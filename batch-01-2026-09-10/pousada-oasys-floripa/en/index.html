Instagram
The feed is already the website. What is missing is the shop under it.
This section sits up here, not down by the footer, on purpose. Today the entire business runs through an Instagram profile: no website, no direct booking, no search presence for their own name, and no way for a guest to check dates at 23:00 without DMing a human.
The proposal is not to replace the feed. It is to give it an address. The same photographs they already make, now with a date picker directly above them — and on a domain that is theirs. Instagram can change the algorithm tomorrow; it cannot change their domain.
