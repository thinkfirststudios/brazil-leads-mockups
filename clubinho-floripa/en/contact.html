The name, qualification and CREF registration of whoever leads the children’s class [CONFIRM]. For a kids’ class that is not a detail: it is the buying decision.

How is my child’s data handled?

A minor’s data has its own rule. LGPD art. 14 requires the processing to be in the child’s best interest and to rest on specific, highlighted consent from the legal guardian. On this site the children’s enrolment starts with the guardian, the consent is separate from everything else, and the child does not go onto a marketing list.

Do you publish photos of the children?

No image on this site shows a child. A photograph of a minor only arrives from the studio, with documented permission from the guardians — and permission can be withdrawn, which means knowing which photo each child is in. [CONFIRM the studio’s policy]

About the children’s class

A children’s jiu-jitsu class means a minor’s data in the records. LGPD art. 14 requires that processing to be in the child’s best interest, with specific, highlighted consent from a parent or legal guardian. That is why the children’s enrolment on this site asks for the guardian before anything else. And no image on this site shows a child: a photograph of a minor only arrives from you, with documented permission, after signature.

No promise of a result. No sentence on this site claims that an activity treats, cures or relieves a condition. Under the CDC (arts. 30 and 37) advertising binds whoever publishes it, and a health-outcome promise is where that bites hardest.

Every photograph is stock, used as a placeholder and labelled as such. None shows Clubinho’s unit, team or a class held there, and no person pictured stands in for a practitioner.
