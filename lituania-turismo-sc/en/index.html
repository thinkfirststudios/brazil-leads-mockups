This site serves two audiences that currently fight over one page. On one side, thirteen dated packages — and eleven of them leave Santa Catarina, sold to people who already live here. On the other, city tours, transfers, receptive services and corporate transport, sold to people arriving. Their current service menu leads with the second and has not one line of content behind it. This build separates them: the calendar lives here, the fleet lives on the transport page.
