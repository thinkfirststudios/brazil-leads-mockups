Before you apply

Who it's for — and who it isn't

Who it's for

  • Already practising, but improvising day to day
  • Working hard and earning little
  • Quoting without confidence
  • Depending on referrals
  • Feeling the business is not under control

Who it isn't for

  • Anyone who doesn't want to put the work in
  • Anyone looking only for isolated tips
  • Anyone unwilling to take responsibility for their own business

The method

Three pillars

01

Business structure

  • The minimum structure of an interiors practice
  • Internal processes: briefing, proposal, contract, delivery
  • Time management and prioritisation
  • Reading the numbers

02

Pricing and sales

  • Strategic pricing
  • Proposals that convert
  • Selling with ease
  • A safer sales process

03

Authorial positioning

  • Instagram as a shop window and authority channel
  • Strategic lead generation
  • A growth strategy
