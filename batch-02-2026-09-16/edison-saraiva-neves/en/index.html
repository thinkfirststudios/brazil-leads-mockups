About Dr. Edison

Edison Saraiva Neves is a general practitioner, researcher, nutrologist and vitalist, unicist homeopath. He graduated in 1976 from UnB – the University of Brasília – the city where he lives and practises medicine today, and he also offers online consultations.

His work is integrative, bringing medicine together with the environment, society, the body, the psyche and the spirit.
