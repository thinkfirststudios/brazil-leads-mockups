Data collected in a proposal request

The corporate proposal form collects a CNPJ, a job title, an approximate traveller count and an annual travel spend band. This is company and travel-programme data, processed on the basis of legitimate interest and of pre-contractual dealings (LGPD, art. 7, items V and IX), solely to prepare and answer the requested proposal. It is not used for marketing without separate, specific consent, and is not passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

Traveller data

In performing a travel management contract we process data on people nominated by the contracting company — name, identity document, date of birth, seat and meal preferences, loyalty programmes and, where an airline, hotel or immigration authority requires it, passport and visa data. This processing is for performance of the contract (art. 7, V) and for compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation (art. 7, II).
