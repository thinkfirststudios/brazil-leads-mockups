Allergens and information

A shared fryer means cross-contact.

Mandatory declaration — ANVISA RDC 26/2015

A per-item declaration, for those that apply. In a seafood pastelaria the minimum set is:

crustaceans (shrimp)fish wheat (gluten)milk eggssoytree nuts

Cross-contact

Where there is a shared fryer, cross-contact is close to certain and the “Pode conter…” declaration is required. That is not small print: for someone with a shellfish allergy it is the difference between being able to eat and not. [CONFIRM]
