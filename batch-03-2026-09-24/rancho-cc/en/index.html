How it works

Two delivery routes, decided by your basket

Rancho is a collective-buying group in Florianópolis. A monthly group purchase opens at the start of the month; in the other weeks, what is left of that purchase is sold by the unit.

Enter your postcode

The site checks whether your address is inside Rancho's local delivery area or will receive shipped goods only.

The basket splits itself

Chilled, fresh and frozen items travel by local delivery. Dry, shelf-stable goods ship nationally. Every basket line says which is which.

About

Consumption as a tool, not an impulse

Rancho connects sustainable producers with a group of responsible consumers through collective purchases, prioritising local, family-grown, artisanal, agroecological, organic and biodegradable products.
