⚠️ There was no privacy policy and no cookie gate

This is the most urgent point in the conversation, which is why it sits at the top. The current site at flatpietra.com.br loads Google Tag Manager (container GTM-MBQ68L5W) and the Asksuite assistant (cdn.asksuite.com/infochat.js, company id pietra-flat) the moment the page opens — before any consent, because no consent mechanism exists in the served HTML, and no privacy policy link exists anywhere. Both set identifiers. That is LGPD exposure, not a technical footnote.

In this mockup both sit behind the consent gate, in separate categories, with “Reject all” the same size as “Accept all” and both non-essential categories unticked by default. Neither is actually loaded here: a mockup does not fire anybody’s container.

What this site stores

One thing: pietra.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device. It reaches no server and disappears when you clear site data.

The forms

There are two: the availability enquiry and the contact form. Both compose a text message and open WhatsApp with it ready, in the language of the page you are on. Neither checks a calendar, neither writes to a server, and neither confirms a booking. You see the message before you send it.
