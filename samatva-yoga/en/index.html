The method

Ashtanga in the Mysore style

The school's primary method, in their words: “individual teaching inside a collective practice, respecting each student's pace”. The practices are “dynamic, with a strong emphasis on the breath and on settling the mind”.

  1. You arrive at your time
    The room is already practising. There is no class starting and finishing together: you come in, roll out your mat and begin. Arrival windows per shala [CONFIRM].
  2. You practise your own sequence
    At your pace and as far as you have got. Someone who started a week ago and someone with ten years share the same room.
  3. The teacher moves from one to the next
    Individual adjustment and instruction inside a full room. That is what “Mysore” means.

Never practised before? Not a problem.

The answer is the school's own, from their FAQ: “No. Our school welcomes all levels... We also run a beginners' group regularly.” The day and time of that beginners' group [CONFIRM].

Yoga is physical activity. Talk to a doctor before starting and tell the teacher about injuries, recent surgery, pregnancy and cardiovascular conditions. The practice does not treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing on this site should be read as therapeutic advice.
