Before anything else
One question that changes the whole engagement
⚠ Your handle begins with a franchise prefix. The only public channel that exists is an Instagram profile behind a login wall, and its name is not simply “pousadabangalosdamole”. That is the first question of the meeting, not the last, because the answer decides whether there is a deal and which deal:
  • If the affiliation is live — branding, rates and distribution may all be contractually restricted. You may be barred from advertising a cheaper direct rate, which is the entire standard pitch. We promise none of it before knowing.
  • If it has ended — handles routinely outlive contracts — you have lost your distribution channel and your brand at the same time, and the urgency is real rather than manufactured.
  • If it never existed — a standard independent, plus a recommendation to fix a handle that currently misleads.
[CONFIRM the franchise relationship — current, former or never]
What we did in the meantime. This mockup is built assuming independence, and that is stated here, on the page, rather than buried in a proposal footnote. No franchise mark appears anywhere — no name, no symbol, no colour. And no “best price, only here” line has been written, because rate parity under a franchise or OTA agreement commonly forbids exactly that.
