    Buying

    What documents do I need to buy?

    ID, CPF, proof of marital status and proof of income where there is financing; on the property side, an up-to-date matricula and clearance certificates. The full list Regente uses today: [CONFIRM]. None of it is sent through this site.

    How does financing work?

    Regente advertises financing and paperwork support. Whether they are a correspondente bancario and for which banks: [CONFIRM]. No rate, instalment or simulation is published without confirmation.

    What costs are there beyond the price?

    ITBI, notary and registration fees, and any financing charges. The percentages change by municipality and by transaction, so no figure appears here without a source. [CONFIRM the current rates in Florianopolis]

    Is the valuation really free?

    The free valuation is a differentiator Regente publishes themselves. Scope and conditions: [CONFIRM]

    Renting

    Which guarantees do you accept?

    The three usual ones in Brazil are a guarantor, a deposit and rent-guarantee insurance, and each changes the cost and the approval time. Which Regente accepts and on what terms: [CONFIRM]. This site does not collect guarantor details.

    What is payable on top of the rent?

    The service charge and IPTU where they apply, plus a management fee where there is one. That is why every rental card on this site shows the three lines separately with a monthly total — advertising the rent alone hides the real cost.
