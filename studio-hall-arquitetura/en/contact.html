A note on the paid traffic

Your sitemap contains /lpgoogle and a live, indexed copy of it at /cópia-lp-google. An indexed duplicate of a paid landing page is a ranking problem and a measurement problem at once: it competes with the original and splits the signals. The fix is a 301 from the copy to the real landing page, coordinated with the campaigns’ final URLs so the ads do not break. Same family of problem: the privacy policy URL is /políticadeprivacidade, with accented characters — here it has become /politica-de-privacidade.
