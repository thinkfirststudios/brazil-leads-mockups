Allergens (Anvisa RDC 26/2015)

  • peanut
  • cashew
  • tree nuts
  • sesame
  • soy
  • fish (fish sauce)
  • crustaceans (shrimp paste)
  • molluscs (oyster sauce)
  • milk
  • egg
  • gluten (soy sauce)
  • sulphites

Fish sauce, shrimp paste, oyster sauce and peanuts are common in Thai cooking and may not be visible in the finished dish. Soy sauce usually contains wheat (gluten). If you have an allergy, tell the team before ordering.

Every dish will carry its full allergen list and the Brazilian gluten statement (Law 10.674/2003). [CONFIRM ingredients dish by dish]
