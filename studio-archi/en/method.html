FAQ

The two answers they already publish

How long will my project take?

The duration depends on several stages, from the initial studies through to municipal approval submissions and the full architectural documentation for construction. At our briefing meeting you will tell us what you need and, together, we will define the level of delivery that best meets your expectations.

Does it have to be in person?

No. We work both in person and remotely. A large share of our projects is developed online, with no technical or operational loss whatsoever. Our methodology ensures the whole process flows with clarity, organisation and security, allowing the work on site to proceed without problems or worries.
