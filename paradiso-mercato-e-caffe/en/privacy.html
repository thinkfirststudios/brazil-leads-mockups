No WhatsApp number was captured — and none has been invented

This is the most practical point in the whole mockup. There is no phone number, no WhatsApp and no e-mail. Rather than drop a stand-in number in, every WhatsApp control in this build is visibly disabled and explains why when tapped. The reason is simple: an invented number on a published site is not a layout detail, it is a stranger’s phone ringing. Confirm the business WhatsApp number and every button on the page works with no other change.

What this site stores

One thing: paradiso.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device, so you are not asked again. It does not leave the device, it reaches no server, and it disappears when you clear site data in your browser.

Nothing third-party loads before consent

There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no Instagram widget and no embedded map — the map in the “Find us” section is an SVG drawn here, precisely so Google is not called without your consent. The photographs come from a stock image CDN. The consent banner exists anyway, with analytics and marketing off by default and “Reject all” carrying the same weight as “Accept all”, so that any future tag arrives through the right door.

The forms

There are three — hampers and orders, booking/pre-order, and the order buttons on the menu. All work the same way: they compose a text message and hand it to WhatsApp. None writes to a server and none sends anything on its own. While there is no confirmed number they send nowhere at all: they show the notice above. [CONFIRM how long conversations are kept and who has access].

Price, product, and what this site refuses to assert

Nothing in the source spreadsheet described this business — not one word — and Instagram returned nothing usable. So:

  • No dish, product, brand, importer, region, producer or DOP/IGP designation has been named. None. The mercato categories are categories, not items.
  • No price has been invented. Every one reads [CONFIRM], in BRL and in the form R$ 00,00. Nothing has been converted to US dollars.
  • No Italian-family origin story has been written. It is the most tempting fabrication available on this lead and the one that collapses fastest under a customer’s first question.
  • No rating, review, testimonial, follower count, founding year, CNPJ, licence or metric appears unmarked. The numbers band ships literally empty.
  • Not even the business format has been assumed. The mockup carries an amber note just below the hero asking whether Paradiso is a deli, a café, a trattoria, or both.
