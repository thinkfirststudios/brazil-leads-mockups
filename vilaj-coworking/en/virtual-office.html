The documentation is the argument

Nobody chooses a fiscal address on price: they choose it because they do not want to discover, halfway through registering a company, that a document is missing. They already have the answer and it is written as a run-on sentence.

“All the documentation required in relation to the address: habite-se comercial, fire-service licences, IPTU, proof of address and other required documents.”

  • Habite-se comercial
  • Fire-service licences
  • IPTU
  • Proof of address
No document has been added to their list. The list above is exactly what they publish. Adding a document type they do not hold would create an expectation that turns into a problem at the commercial registry counter.
