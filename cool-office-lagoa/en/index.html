Why Lagoa

The best coworking address in Florianópolis.

Lagoa da Conceição is the kitesurf and stand-up paddle centre of Florianópolis and the island’s densest concentration of remote workers. Restaurants, gyms, banks and the bus hub are all walkable. Praia Mole and Joaquina are a short ride away.

It is also what people type: coworking Lagoa da Conceição. Today that address is Cool Office’s most valuable asset — and it is not working anywhere on the internet.
