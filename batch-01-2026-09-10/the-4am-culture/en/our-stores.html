The country selector

Pick Germany and watch what happens

Your country selector lists about forty countries — Germany, the United States, Argentina, Portugal, Japan — and serves every one of them the same Portuguese page, priced in reais. The decision to sell abroad has already been made and the selector has already been built. The localisation behind it has not.

This mockup ships three real trees — /, /en/ and /es/ — with genuinely translated pages and reciprocal hreflang, not a JavaScript text swap. Spanish is here because Argentina and Uruguay are already in your own country list, and because Floripa’s summer Argentine population walks past your Florianópolis store.
