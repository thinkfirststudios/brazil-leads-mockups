How to read the product cards

  • In store now / To order — whether the item is on the shelf or needs ordering in.
  • Certification mark — a slot reserved for a real, verifiable mark. No certificate, no mark.
  • Dietary marks (gluten-free, lactose-free, vegan, no added sugar) — only what the manufacturer's label states. [CONFIRM per product]
