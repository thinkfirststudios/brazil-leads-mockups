Skin, allergies and pregnancy

Recommending a cosmetic well means knowing who it is for. The problem is not the conversation at the counter — it is what happens after it: the customer record, the WhatsApp history, the recommendations spreadsheet. If the information is kept, it needs specific consent, a written purpose and a retention period. If it is not kept, better still — and that is worth telling the customer.

What must never happen: using a skin condition to segment a campaign, build an ad audience or feed an automated recommendation. [CONFIRM what is kept today and where]

Two web addresses, and what that means for the data

The domain the press points at, terramayacosmetica.com.br, does not resolve. A shop exists at terramaya.com.br and we could not confirm it is the same legal entity.

That is not only a marketing question. If they are two companies, they are two controllers: someone who bought from one is not the other’s data subject, the databases cannot be merged into one mailing list, and each answers for its own art. 18 requests. Settling who owns which address comes before any campaign goes out. [CONFIRM]

Exchanges, withdrawal and warranty

A purchase made away from the premises — website, WhatsApp, social media — carries seven days of withdrawal under CDC art. 49, counted from receipt. An opened cosmetic is the classic edge case and the policy has to be written down before the purchase, not at the moment of the exchange. [CONFIRM the exchange policy]

The statutory warranty is 30 days for non-durable goods, which covers most cosmetics (CDC art. 26, I). Expiry date and batch belong to the manufacturer and must be legible on the label.

Product claims

Cosmetics are regulated by ANVISA and cannot carry a therapeutic claim: to treat, cure or relieve a symptom changes the product’s category. “Organic”, “vegan” and “cruelty free” depend on certification per product, and an environmental claim has to be provable under CONAR’s standard. Under the CDC (arts. 30 and 37), whatever is published binds the shop. [CONFIRM product by product]
