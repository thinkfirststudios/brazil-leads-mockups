Third-party data: the testimonials section

This is the part almost no architecture site in Brazil covers. The testimonials section publishes the names, job titles and employers of real people who are not visitors to this site and never filled in a form. That is third-party personal data processing under the LGPD and it needs its own lawful basis — in practice, written consent from each named person (art. 7, item I), obtained before publication and revocable at any time (art. 8, § 5).

A testimonial standing on one website is not automatically a licence to publish it on another. Naming the company as a client is a separate permission from the individual’s permission, and logo use is a third thing again, requiring a trademark licence. Project confidentiality

Corporate, industrial and healthcare projects are frequently under non-disclosure agreements, and plant layouts, clinic plans and site plans can be security-sensitive information, not merely commercially reserved. No image, plan, section or BIM model view is published without a specific written release, project by project. [CONFIRM]

Photography
