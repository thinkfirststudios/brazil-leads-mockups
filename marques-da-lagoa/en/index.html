Menu

Sharing, first.

The hierarchy is inverted on purpose: a waterfront bar sells food to share between people watching the sun go down, not starter-main-dessert.

No dish, no drink and no price has been invented. Nothing was recoverable from Instagram, so every card is a visible placeholder. The allergen chips are the categories ANVISA RDC 26/2015 requires to be declared — not a claim about any dish. [CONFIRM the full menu, prices in R$, how many people each porção serves, and per-item allergens]
How many it serves. On a sharing menu this is not a courtesy: under the Brazilian Consumer Code the price must be clear, and the number of people served sitting next to the price is the biggest single cause of an argument over the bill.
