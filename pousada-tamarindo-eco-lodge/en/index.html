Sustainability
The part of this business that is hidden on a sub-page
This is the only lead in the batch that can actually substantiate its eco claim — and on the current site all of it sits on a sub-page, as running text, with not one image. Every line below is theirs, word for word.
  • Demolition-wood furniture
    “In every unit the furniture was designed and built exclusively for the lodge, all of it made from demolition wood.”
  • Seven low blocks
    “We kept it horizontal and divided the buildings into 07 distinct blocks, distributed harmoniously across the site, to minimise the visual impact.”
  • Cross ventilation on the wind axis
    Every unit designed with cross ventilation on a north/south axis, which corresponds to “98% of the winds at Campeche beach”. Openings face north to maximise daylight, and upper-floor units have double-height ceilings.
  • LED with photocells and motion sensors
    External and common-area lighting in LED with photocells and motion sensors; LED inside the units too, except where it would spoil the room.
  • Electric showers, and the reasoning
    They publish why they chose electric showers over solar: Florianópolis’s high seasonality, the colder climate, the low solar incidence for most of the year in the south of Brazil, and the water savings. That is an unusual piece of transparency and it deserves to be seen.
  • Central minibar
    In-room fridges exist but are switched off by default, with all the drinks in a single fridge at reception. Anyone who wants theirs on can switch it on.
  • Linen
    Towels changed every two days and sheets every four, to control effluent.
  • Waste
    Selective collection on site, with a dedicated communal separation space.
  • Local suppliers
    Breakfast made with regional seasonal produce and traditional local family recipes; suppliers “sourced entirely from local suppliers”, chosen partly for their own sustainability commitment.
  • A sustainability fee since 10/2020
    “From October 2020 we decided to create a sustainability fee, which is allocated 100% to sustainable purposes.” [CONFIRM the amount and how it is disclosed at booking — a mandatory fee has to appear before checkout]

A technical note
Three template strings live on the site
Put carefully, because it is nobody’s fault in particular: today the homepage and the Sustainability page display, as visible text inside their sliders, the strings “Escreva sua legenda aqui”, “Título do Slide” and “Button”.
