FAQ

What people ask before signing up

Is access really 24/7?

For members, yes — through biometric entry. Staffed reception is Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours the space is open to anyone on a plan, but there is no team at the desk.

What is a “período”?

A five-hour block: morning, 08:00–13:00 or afternoon, 13:00–18:00. Hot-desk plans count periods, not days and not loose hours.

Why is the call booth capped at 3h a day?

So everyone gets a turn. It is their rule and it is on their site: booths are booked online and limited to 3h per person per day. Because it is a contractual term it appears beside the plan rather than in a footnote.

How do I book a meeting room?

Online, by members. The included hours vary by plan — they are in the comparison table above. Hours beyond the allowance are charged separately, with a discount depending on the plan. [CONFIRM the extra-hour rate]

Can drop-in users book a meeting room?

[CONFIRM] — the current site says the call booths are bookable by members or by drop-in desk users, but does not say the same about the rooms. Nothing has been assumed here.

Is there parking?

Yes, at MULTI Open Shopping, which has a large car park. Private-office plans include 1 space. Whether there is validation for visitors and for other plans, [CONFIRM].

Can I bring my dog?

You can — Tarrafa is pet friendly, and that is on their site. House rules, accepted sizes and whether any area is off-limits [CONFIRM].

What does the fiscal address include — and exclude?

It includes a fiscal address, a commercial address and unlimited mail receipt. And there is an exclusion that is theirs: on the Tubarão Cação annual plan, the Fiscal Address fee is not included. Registering a company at the address depends on supporting documentation and a separate contract. [CONFIRM the terms]
