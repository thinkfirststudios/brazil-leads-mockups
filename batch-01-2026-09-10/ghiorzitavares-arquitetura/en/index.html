The process

Six phases, from briefing to handover.

Written by you, and reproduced word for word. It is the best thing on the current site — and today it sits buried below everything else.

The process page →
01

Briefing

An initial meeting to understand your hopes, purposes and intentions, along with the projected investment for the design, the works and any complementary services.

02

Preliminary Study

After the briefing and the costing, we present the design to be agreed with you, using photorealistic images from the 3D BIM model.

03

Permitting

Our team handles every bureaucratic step with the relevant authorities, to legalise the project and obtain the building permit.

04

Technical Design

An assembly manual, with details of every construction component and quantities for all the materials required.

05

Construction

Our team acts between you, the trades and the suppliers, and construction then begins.

06

Site Supervision

Site visits while the works progress, with direct contact between the teams and you, resolving any design questions that arise.
