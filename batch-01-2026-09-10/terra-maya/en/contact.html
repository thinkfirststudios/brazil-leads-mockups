Where you are on the web

Three addresses, and the main one does not exist

This section does not usually belong on a shop’s website. It belongs on this one because it is the most expensive item on the list and nobody seems to have noticed.

The domain in the press does not resolve

terramayacosmetica.com.br returns NXDOMAIN. It is not a site that is down: the domain does not exist in DNS. Every article and every directory still sends people there, and those people arrive nowhere.

A shop exists on another domain

terramaya.com.br answers and sells cosmetics, footwear and accessories, with the line “Cosmetics that respect your skin — and the planet”. Florianópolis appears nowhere on it. [CONFIRM whether it is yours]

Two Instagram profiles

The record we were given carries @terramayacuradoria; the press points to @terramayacosmetica. Two profiles split the audience, the history and the social proof down the middle. [CONFIRM which one stays]

The order of the fix matters. First find out whether the dead domain can be recovered — if it can, it comes back and redirects to the new one, and five years of press links start working again. If it cannot, the next best thing is to settle on a single address and correct the directories one by one. Neither is design work, and both are worth more than any new page.

Are all the products vegan?

This site does not claim that.
