Allergens and information

Allergen declaration

ANVISA RDC 26/2015 requires food allergens to be declared. It applies to the à la carte menu and to event menus.

GlutenMilkEggsSoyTree nutsPeanutsFishCrustaceansMolluscs
Cross-contact. A kitchen producing many dishes on the same bench carries a cross-contact risk, and that has to be said before the order. The exact wording the kitchen can stand behind: [CONFIRM]
At an event this is money, not just compliance. Whoever plans an event is asked to guarantee dietary accommodation for a whole guest list. A venue that publishes this wins the booking from one that does not.

What is the capacity of the space?

It is the number-one question about any events venue and the answer is published nowhere. Seated and standing are different numbers and both matter. [CONFIRM seated and standing capacity] No figure was estimated here: a wrong capacity costs you the booking after the deposit.

Can the whole space be hired?

Exclusive hire — a buyout — changes everything about the quote and the guest experience. [CONFIRM whether the space is hired exclusively or whether events run alongside normal service]

Does the restaurant close on event days?

A small question with a large frustration behind it: a guest arrives for dinner and finds a private party. [CONFIRM] Publishing it is the mark of a venue paying attention.

What does an event cost?

It depends on the format, the guest count and the date, and no amount was given to the agency. [CONFIRM the model: per person, minimum spend, room hire, or a combination] When a “from” price is published it has to be genuinely achievable and to carry the mandatory extras beside it.

Can I bring my own caterer?

How do the deposit and cancellation work?
