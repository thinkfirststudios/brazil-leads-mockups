For tenants

Rental guarantees in Brazil

Brazil's Tenancy Law (Lei 8.245/91) allows one type of guarantee per lease. Types Buzz accepts: [CONFIRM].

Fiador (guarantor)

A person contractually takes on the tenant's obligations if the tenant defaults. The guarantor usually proves income and owns property.

Caução (deposit)

The tenant pays a deposit at the start of the lease. A cash deposit is limited by law to three months' rent.

Seguro-fiança (rent guarantee insurance)

An insurer guarantees payment to the landlord if the tenant defaults. The tenant pays the premium after a credit check. Costs: [CONFIRM]

Título de capitalização

The tenant buys a capitalisation bond pledged to the lease. With no arrears at the end, it can be redeemed under the bond's rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is a neighbourhood data sheet?
A table of descriptive facts per neighbourhood — Buzz's current listings, access, infrastructure and built form — with the source and update date for every figure. It contains no value projections.
Are prices shown in US dollars?
No. All prices are published in Brazilian reais (R$).
Can foreigners buy property in Brazil?
Requirements and documents (e.g. a CPF tax number): [CONFIRM — lawyer-reviewed text]
Who pays the condo fee and IPTU?
Under Brazil's Tenancy Law, ordinary condominium expenses fall to the tenant and extraordinary ones to the landlord; IPTU may be passed to the tenant if the lease says so.
