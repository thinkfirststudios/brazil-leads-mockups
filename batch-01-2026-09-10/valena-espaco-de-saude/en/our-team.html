Their eight category pages do not list a single individual treatment, so this build ships the category structure with a visible placeholder row — and no botox, filler, laser, cryolipolysis or microneedling, because none of it appears on their site. [CONFIRM the treatment list for each area, with duration and the responsible professional]
Why there is no price on any card. CFM Resolution 1.974/2011 prohibits publishing prices, promotions and discounts for medical procedures. Until it is confirmed, area by area, which offerings are medical procedures and which are wellness services, the safe build shows no price at all — and carries no discount bar, no “from R$” and no countdown. That is protection, not timidity: building it correctly is cheaper than being told to take it down. [CONFIRM the medical / non-medical split]
