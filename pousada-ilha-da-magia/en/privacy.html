What this site stores

One thing: ilhadamagia.consent.v1, your cookie choice, in localStorage on this device. It reaches no server and disappears when you clear site data. No third-party resource loads before consent — the map in the location section is an SVG drawn here, precisely so Google is not called without consent.

The forms, and what the availability enquiry actually does

There are three: the availability enquiry, the contact form and the WhatsApp buttons (booking, breakfast and tourism desk). All work the same way: they compose a text message and open WhatsApp with it ready, in the language of the page you are on.

Worth being explicit about one thing, because it is a point of the conversation: the availability enquiry checks no calendar and shows no rate. It asks. The testimonials

The eight names in the reviews section are the ones already published on their own site, exactly as they appear there. Prices

No nightly rate appears in this build because none is published: every card carries a visible [CONFIRM].
