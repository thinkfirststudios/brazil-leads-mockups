Caring for your piece

How to make swimwear last more than one season

Rinse in fresh water, dry in the shade, do not wring, do not soak in chlorine, and keep it away from direct contact with sunscreen. Genuinely useful, and it says more about quality than any adjective.

Delivery, exchanges and returns

The 7-day right of regret, stated in full

A purchase made remotely — including over WhatsApp — carries a 7-day right of regret (CDC art. 49). The hygiene restriction on swimwear exists, but it does not defeat that right on an unworn piece with its hygiene strip intact. It is the most misapplied rule in the category.
