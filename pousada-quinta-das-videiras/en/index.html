The Buildings
Three buildings, and today one paragraph
This is the property’s most distinctive asset and on the current site it occupies a single line of body copy. Here it becomes a three-panel sequence, with their own description on each.
  • Casarão Lisboa
    “A homage to the Portuguese colonisation of Florianópolis, it presents an authentic Portuguese style that recovers the local tradition.”
    Their own description, verbatim
  • Solarium
    “At the centre of the gardens and near the pool stands the elegant Solarium, with coloured stained glass and a structure imported from Vietnam, evoking the charm of the Palácio de Cristal in Petrópolis.”
    Their own description, verbatim
  • Casa Porto
    “Recently, the hotel was enriched with the imposing Casa Porto, a Portuguese-style building with meticulously preserved details and finishes.”
    Their own description, verbatim
The three panels above are drawings, not photographs. There is no honest stock image of a Portuguese colonial manor in Florianópolis, of a stained-glass pavilion whose frame came from Vietnam, or of a Porto townhouse with preserved finishes — and putting the wrong building on the page would be worse than drawing one. This is the section that most needs your own photography, and it should end up with zero stock images.
