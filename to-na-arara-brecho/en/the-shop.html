FAQ

What people ask before reserving

How do I reserve a piece?

Tap the tag and WhatsApp opens with the piece number already in the message — no need to explain which dress, no screenshot, no “the one from Tuesday”. [CONFIRM the shop’s WhatsApp — no number is dialled in this mockup]

How long is a reservation held?

[CONFIRM]. Whatever the window, it has to be written on the tag and be the same for everyone — that is what prevents the who-asked-first conversation.

What does each condition grade mean?

The scale has five points and it is published in full on the condition page. The rule that makes it real: every piece below As New carries a close-up photograph of the mark. [CONFIRM the shop’s real grades]

Are the clothes washed before sale?

[CONFIRM]. Nothing has been asserted here, because “higienizada” is a claim and not a mood: if it is said, it has to be true and described.

Can I return something that does not fit?

Exchanging for another size does not exist here — there is only one unit of each piece. What does exist is the 7-day right of regret (CDC art. 49) on any remote purchase, with a full refund including outbound shipping. And the best defence against a piece not fitting is measuring first: the measurements are on every tag, and you can ask the shop to measure again.

Do you ship?

A secondary source says yes, across Brazil. Carrier, cost, times and tracking [CONFIRM]. Shipping makes this a distance sale, and the whole CDC regime comes with it.
