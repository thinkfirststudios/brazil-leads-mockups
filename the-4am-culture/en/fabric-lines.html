On the discounts, as a question rather than a correction. Four Morning Sale tiers (10/20/30/40%), the PRIMEIRA4AM coupon, a LAST CALL flag on the sweatshirts and the free-shipping threshold all run at once. That is legal, and three things need checking: (a) every discounted price must show the genuine prior price, and it must actually have been charged; (b) “on selected items” must be resolvable — a customer has to be able to see which; (c) strategically, a brand whose thesis is engineered fabric and community is spending its homepage on price. This mockup has not “fixed” any of that — it has put the question. [CONFIRM]

Fit and sizing

The question of fit

In activewear the purchase decision is one thing: will it fit, and will it hold? This is the layer that reduces returns and the one the current site does not have in any usable form — and you are already paying for a returns platform.

  • Model reference: “the model is 1.7X m and wears size M” [CONFIRM the height and size of each model, per photograph]
  • Between two sizes: the recommendation changes per line — a Compress and a Soft do not behave the same. [CONFIRM the recommendation for each line]
  • See-through. It is the most-asked question about light-coloured leggings, and answering it honestly is a trust asset, not a risk. Each line needs its own opacity note. [CONFIRM line by line]
  • Rise, per style [CONFIRM]
