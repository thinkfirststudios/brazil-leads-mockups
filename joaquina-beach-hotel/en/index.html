The Hotel

Simple, clean, and a hundred metres from the sea.

“O Joaquina Beach Hotel está localizado na praia da Joaquina, a poucos metros do mar, na costa leste da ilha de Santa Catarina, a 20 km do centro da cidade, 15 km do aeroporto…” — their own words: a few metres from the sea, on the east coast of Santa Catarina island, 20 km from the city centre, 15 km from the airport, and 2 km from Lagoa da Conceição.

Your own guests describe the hotel as “acomodações simples, mas bem limpas e suficientes para praia” and “em frente ao mar, por um preço excelente”. That is a position, not a flaw — and this site is built to own it rather than pretend to be a resort.
