Sessions, house rules and what to expect from the cats — so the visit is good for you and for them.

How a visit works

You are a guest in their home

A cat café isn’t an ordinary café: the cats live here, sleep when they like and choose who they approach. These rules exist for them.

Please do

  • Watch and wait for a cat to approach
  • Speak softly and move calmly
  • Ask the team about each cat
