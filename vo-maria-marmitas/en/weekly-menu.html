Real food

The counterweight to the logistics

Half of this business is logistics and the other half is home cooking. The page has to hold both — and this is the half that makes someone choose you over an app.

Every sentence in this section is a claim. “No preservatives”, “fresh ingredients”, “cooked to order” and “real food” are assertions about the product, not brand mood — and none has been written here because none is confirmed. [CONFIRM: fresh or frozen, preservatives, and where the ingredients come from]

Lines and diets

What each line excludes

Low carb, fit, vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free — all are common in this category and none has been listed here, because which ones exist is unknown.

[CONFIRM which lines exist] and, for each, exactly what it excludes — “fit” and “low carb” mean different things to different people, and in a product someone eats five times a week the definition matters.
No calorie and no macro figure without real data. Publishing a nutrition table requires a standardised recipe or lab analysis. If it exists, it is a strong differentiator for the fit audience and deserves a proper table. If it does not, no number is published. [CONFIRM whether nutritional information exists]
