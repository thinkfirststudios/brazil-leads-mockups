⚠️ The medical disclosure is sensitive personal data

This is the most important point in this policy and the one that needs legal review before anything goes live at all. A kite school needs to know, before the first lesson, whether a student has a cardiac condition, epilepsy, is pregnant, has a shoulder or knee injury, or has had recent surgery. That is sensitive personal data under LGPD art. 11. In practice:

  • It needs specific, separate, explicit consent — it cannot ride along with the general acceptance on a contact form.
  • It needs a specific, stated purpose: assessing fitness for the activity, and nothing beyond that.
  • It needs a stricter access regime — only those who need to see it, see it — and a defined retention period, with disposal at the end.
  • It cannot be a web field feeding an ordinary inbox. The contact form in this build deliberately does not collect health data, and the note beneath it says so.

Prices and cancellation

No price appears in this build, because none is published. Under the Brazilian consumer code a price must be total, unambiguous and state exactly what it includes — how many hours, over how many days, whether equipment and insurance are in, and what happens to the hours if the wind dies. Cancellation and rescheduling terms for no wind, too much wind, storms or lightning must be published before any course is sellable. [CONFIRM the price table and the cancellation policy].
